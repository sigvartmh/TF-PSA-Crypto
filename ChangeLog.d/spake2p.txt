Features
   * Add support for the SPAKE2+ password-authenticated key exchange
     (RFC 9383) through the PSA PAKE API. The HMAC and CMAC cipher suites
     are supported on the NIST P-256, P-384 and P-521 curves, as well as
     the Matter (CHIP) SPAKE2+ variant on P-256.
   * Support SPAKE2+ registration by deriving a SPAKE2+ key pair with
     psa_key_derivation_output_key(), following the deterministic
     procedure described in RFC 9383.
